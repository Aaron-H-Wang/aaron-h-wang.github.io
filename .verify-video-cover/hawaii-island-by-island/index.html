Journey to Hawaiʻi

Dec 31, 2025 · Photo essay

Four islands, many changing skies, and a camera close at hand. These are a few memories from Hawaiʻi in December 2025.

01 · LAND OF FIRE

Big Island

Black lava, restless earth, and the last orange light falling into the Pacific.

02 · ABOVE THE CLOUDS

Maui

Haleakalā at sunset, then wind and waves along the northern shore.

03 · THE GARDEN ISLE

Kauaʻi

Canyons cut into red earth, with green valleys disappearing into rain and mist.

04 · RIDGES & OCEAN

Oʻahu

Rainbows on the trail, blue water beyond the ridges, and quiet encounters near Honolulu.

A final roll of ocean blue, volcanic black, and December light—one journey I will remember for a long time.
