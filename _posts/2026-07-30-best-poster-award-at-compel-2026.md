<p class="research-intro">We are delighted to share that our paper received the <strong>Best Poster Award</strong> at the 27th IEEE Workshop on Control and Modeling for Power Electronics (COMPEL 2026), held in Cambridge, United Kingdom.</p>

The award recognizes our paper, **“Multi-Resonant Load-independent ZVS Network with Quasi-Spike Current,”** authored by **Ziyang Xu, Yihao Wu, Haoyu Wang, and Alex Hanson**.

<figure class="research-figure research-figure--wide">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/posts/compel-2026-best-poster-award.png' | relative_url }}" alt="COMPEL 2026 Best Poster Award certificate presented to Ziyang Xu, Yihao Wu, Haoyu Wang, and Alex Hanson.">
  <figcaption>Best Poster Award presented at COMPEL 2026, Cambridge, United Kingdom, July 20-24, 2026.</figcaption>
</figure>

This project explores a multi-resonant power-conversion network designed to achieve load-independent zero-voltage switching while producing a quasi-spike current waveform. Receiving this recognition from the COMPEL community is a meaningful encouragement for our continuing work in high-frequency power electronics.

Most importantly, **congratulations to Ziyang** on this well-deserved achievement. His dedication to the research and presentation played a central role in bringing the work to the conference stage. It has been a pleasure to contribute to the project alongside Yihao and Prof. Hanson, and we are grateful to everyone who supported the work.
